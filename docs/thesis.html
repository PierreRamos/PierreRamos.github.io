Overview

Dynamic difficulty adjustment tunes a game's challenge while it's being played, so it keeps up with the player instead of relying on a fixed difficulty setting. For my undergraduate thesis I designed and built an arcade shooter that does this with Monte Carlo Tree Search.

The thesis was defended in 2023 as part of my BS in Computer Science, major in Game Development. The paper isn't published online.
